## How much rock do I need?

**Calculate the volume of the layer you are filling, then apply the matching bulk weight.**

A “how much rock do I need?” calculator starts with square feet and depth, not the name of the rock alone. Cubic yards = area × (depth in inches / 12) / 27. Tons = cubic yards × tons per cubic yard. Split an irregular bed into measured sections and add them rather than guessing one width.

## How does a river rock calculator differ from a crushed rock calculator?

**The geometry is the same, but the product weight and intended use differ.**

Rounded river rock and angular crushed rock have different shapes and grading. Two Rivers lists 3/4-inch washed round rock at about 1.3 US short tons per yard and 5/8-inch crushed top course at about 1.4. Three Z gives about 1.4 for its washed river gravel family. These are named supplier references rather than a single weight for every river stone. A “how much river rock do I need?” calculator needs the rock size, coverage depth and the actual product factor.

## What affects a landscape rock calculator result?

**Depth, grading, moisture and the entered allowance affect the result.**

The measured depth represents the rock layer rather than every material underneath it. Choose the supplier reference that matches the rock on your quote. Zero allowance leaves volume unadjusted; it does not state that installation has no settling or material loss. Large decorative pieces and boulders need a supplier-specific coverage method rather than an automatic small-gravel assumption.

## What needs checking before ordering rock?

**Confirm the selected product, its sale unit and the rounded order quantity.**

Ask whether the price is per ton, loose cubic yard, bag or container. Enter the supplier’s purchase increment and truck capacity when those apply.
