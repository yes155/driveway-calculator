## How much does a concrete driveway cost?

**The cost of concrete driveway installation starts with area multiplied by an installed square-foot range, rather than the delivered concrete price alone.**

HomeGuide’s standard range is $6–$10 per square foot. Concrete Network lists plain gray concrete at $5–$8, while Angi separates plain finishes at $6–$8 from broom finishes at $8–$12. These scopes differ; Driveway Calculators uses HomeGuide’s named standard benchmark without averaging them. Compare the concrete slab, slab thickness, reinforcement, formwork, finish, control joints, and subbase described in each proposal.

## How much is a concrete driveway per square foot?

**The price per square foot includes installation work, whereas a price per cubic yard describes the ready-mix material.**

Slab thickness changes cubic yards. Rebar and wire mesh describe reinforcement; the quote identifies the reinforcement specified for the job. Ask whether formwork, subbase preparation, curing, sealing, and hauling are included. ## How much does it cost to replace a concrete driveway?

**Replacement combines removal with a new slab, unless the contractor’s installed quote already includes demolition.**
