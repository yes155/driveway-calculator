## How much does a concrete driveway cost?

**HomeGuide’s standard gray concrete driveway benchmark is $6–$10 per square foot installed.**

Concrete Network lists plain gray concrete at $5–$8, while Angi separates plain finishes at $6–$8 from broom finishes at $8–$12. These scopes differ; Driveway Calculators uses HomeGuide’s named standard benchmark without averaging them. A “cement driveway” refers to the same concrete installation scope in this guide.

## Which work does the installed rate cover?

**The installed rate covers concrete placement and finishing; the quote identifies the rest of the job scope.**

Compare the cost of concrete driveway work using the slab thickness, reinforcement, formwork, finish, control joints and subbase in each proposal. Ask whether subbase preparation, curing, sealing and hauling are included. Rebar and wire mesh describe reinforcement; the quote identifies the reinforcement specified for the job. The [Concrete Driveway Cost Calculator](/concrete-driveway-cost-calculator/) applies your measured area to the selected installed rate.

## How does replacement change the project total?

**Replacement adds demolition and disposal to the new-concrete installation scope when those charges are excluded from the installation quote.**

The [Cost to Replace a Concrete Driveway](/replace-concrete-driveway-cost/) explains demolition, disposal, and the new pour. The [Concrete vs Asphalt Driveway](/concrete-vs-asphalt-driveway/) compares the surface options under stated maintenance and lifespan assumptions. The [Stamped Concrete Driveway Cost](/stamped-concrete-driveway-cost/) separates pattern and color pricing from a plain finish.

## Can I pour my own concrete driveway?

**Yes, but a concrete driveway requires placement and finishing skills; Concrete Network recommends professional installation.**

Its DIY guide identifies drainage, subgrade preparation, formwork, jointing and curing as responsibilities the owner takes on. Confirm the concrete specification, site access and crew before ordering.
