## What determines driveway drainage and the required slope?

**The finished elevations, runoff route and local requirements determine the design.**

Do not use one national slope number for every driveway. A cross-slope or crown moves water across the surface; a longitudinal grade describes the driveway’s rise along its length. FHWA’s 4–5 percent crown guidance applies to unpaved roads, not a universal residential driveway rule. The driveway calculator’s slope input adjusts measured geometry; it does not approve a drainage design.

## When does a trench drain suit the problem?

**A surface channel intercepts runoff where water flows across its inlet.**

NDS describes capturing driveway surface water and conveying it to a suitable discharge point. Position, inlet capacity, outlet level and maintenance access belong in the design. A French drain addresses a different subsurface drainage problem; selecting one does not replace the need to understand where surface runoff goes.

## How does a culvert relate to the driveway?

**A culvert carries drainage through an approved crossing when that configuration is needed.**

The route, outlet and road authority requirements control the crossing. Obtain the site-specific pipe specification and permit requirements rather than adopting a nationwide diameter. Protect nearby properties and building entrances when changing the runoff path.

## What does channel installation cost?

**HomeGuide’s driveway-specific range is $400–$2,400 per project.**

Other researched scopes differ: Angi reports $3,000–$9,000 and WSC reports $400–$2,500. The guide retains these differences instead of averaging them. An installed project price is not the price of a manufacturer’s kit alone; confirm excavation, outlet connection, restoration and cleanup.

## Which pavement defects need drainage checked first?

**Persistent pooling, eroding edges and repeated soft areas require the water route to be assessed.**

Standing runoff softens support and contributes to potholes or settling. Clear blocked inlets and document where water collects before ordering surface repairs. The driveway repair guide separates surface treatments from support problems; the gravel construction guide explains why grading and renewal belong together.

## How does driveway slope affect drainage?

**Driveway slope directs surface water along a grade; a channel drain intercepts runoff before the garage.** NDS distinguishes collection capacity and the discharge route. A longitudinal driveway grade differs from a cross-slope or crown. Measure the actual grade and verify the drainage design for the site; public-road guidance is not a universal residential requirement.
