## What gets checked before preparation?

**Identify the exact product, pavement condition, and application weather.**

A search for how to seal coat a driveway brings products with different instructions. Latex-ite Ultra Shield requires two thin coats; Henry HE200’s linked sheet specifies one. Do not merge their directions. Confirm the current label and inspect the driveway for potholes, connected cracking, and persistent water. Sealing is not a correction for structural failure or poor drainage.

## How is the surface prepared?

**Remove debris and complete the repairs required by the selected label.**

Keep crack filling separate from applying a full-area sealer. Repair products have their own dimensional limits and drying instructions. Primer use depends on the named sealer and the stain or repair involved; no universal primer is prescribed. Gather the matching application tool, such as the labeled squeegee or roller, and keep the planned work area clear.

## How are the coats and material quantity planned?

**Use measured area and the named product’s coverage for each required coat.**

The sealcoat calculator uses those inputs to generate the order quantity. Latex-ite Ultra Shield lists a 4.75-gallon pail and different coverage for smooth versus rough asphalt. A second coat does not justify copying another brand’s recoat interval. Mix and apply according to the purchased product, and avoid treating ready-mixed sealer as a concentrate that requires a generic dilution.

## When is the driveway ready for traffic?

**The chosen product’s curing and weather instructions govern reopening.**

For example, Henry HE200’s linked sheet specifies at least 50°F and rising, avoids rain or frost for 48 hours, and allows vehicle use after 24–48 hours under normal conditions. Those values belong to that product alone. Anyone checking how to seal a driveway or how to seal driveway surfaces needs the current package directions. The sealer guide assists selection, the crack filler guide assists preparation, and the cost guide compares professional application with a material-only DIY purchase.

Cleaning and crack preparation precede coating. The product’s application temperature and dry-weather instructions govern the work.
