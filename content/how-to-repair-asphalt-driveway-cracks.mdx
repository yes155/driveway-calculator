## How do you identify the repair first?

**Measure the crack and inspect the surrounding pavement before selecting a product.**

A narrow isolated crack differs from a pothole, sinking, or connected alligator cracking. Photograph the affected area and record whether water remains nearby. Blacktop Concepts explains that structural problems and drainage failures require attention beneath the surface. Instructions on how to repair asphalt driveway damage therefore start with identifying its condition, not with purchasing one filler for every defect.

## Which filler fits a narrow crack?

**Choose the labeled product for the pavement type and measured width.**

Latex-ite 2X is a cold-applied asphalt crack filler for cracks up to 1/2 inch wide. Its manufacturer instructs clearing debris, shaking the bottle, and dispensing through the spout. The measured width identifies whether that example fits; it is not a claim that the product is best. Keep the product’s cure time and weather instructions with your repair plan.

## What changes when the damage is a pothole?

**A pothole requires patch material and compaction rather than a narrow-crack dispensing method.**

QUIKRETE Permanent Blacktop Repair has its own thickness and repair-size limits. Sakrete Blacktop Patch has different placement limits, so instructions from the products are not interchangeable. The asphalt cold patch guide explains those differences. Follow the selected bag’s layer instructions and compact each layer before adding material; the repair remains a product-specific procedure.

## When does professional assessment take priority?

**Assessment takes priority when cracks connect, the surface sinks, or patching repeatedly fails.**

A filler does not establish a correction for weak support. The question of how to repair cracks in an asphalt driveway needs the same separation between surface treatment and foundation repair. Angi reports professional crack filling at $1–$3 per linear foot; the repair cost guide adds scope and minimum-fee context. Finish repairs and respect their product instructions before sealing.
