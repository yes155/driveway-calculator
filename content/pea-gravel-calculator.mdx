## How do you calculate pea gravel?

**Multiply the measured footprint by depth in feet and divide by 27 for cubic yards.**

Calculating pea gravel uses the same volume formula as other aggregate, but its weight factor belongs to the actual small rounded product. A “how much pea gravel do I need?” calculator needs layer depth as well as length and width. Measure separate sections for curves or a widening bed.

## Which density belongs to pea gravel?

**Use the product’s listed bulk weight rather than a generic crushed-stone assumption.**

Two Rivers lists 1/2-inch washed pea gravel at about 2,600 lb per cubic yard, equivalent to 1.3 US short tons. Lasalle’s pea gravel factor is 1.35; Three Z’s #8 washed river gravel is about 1.4. The tool keeps the selected supplier visible and density editable. Moisture and grading change actual delivered weight.

## How do you calculate quantities for paths and patios?

**Calculate only the pea-gravel surface layer, with its own footprint and depth.**

Paths and patios contain different underlying layers, so the surface order excludes their base unless you calculate it separately. A quantity result does not establish a structural base design or certify pea gravel for vehicle loading. Enter an allowance only when your measurement contingency or supplier’s loose-to-finished adjustment is known. Zero excludes extra quantity; it is not a blanket construction recommendation.

## Does this pea gravel cost calculator include installation?

**Material quantity and an entered supplier price describe the rock purchase, not an installed driveway quote.**

Compare the rounded tons or cubic yards with the price unit on the supplier quote. Confirm delivery fees, minimum order and drop access separately.
