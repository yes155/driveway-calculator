## How is a quantity calculated?

**Area, depth, density, and explicitly entered allowances determine the quantity.**

All visible worked examples call the same engine as the interactive tools. Volume in cubic yards follows measured square feet multiplied by depth in inches, divided by 12 and 27. A US short ton is 2,000 lb. Purchase rounding is separate from the calculated material quantity. Bag yield and sealcoat coverage follow a named product label.

## How are prices selected?

**Each selected benchmark retains one publisher’s original range and scope.**

Cost guides and independent contractor pages are compared rather than averaged. Asphalt, concrete, gravel, and paver installation scopes differ. Local supplier lists retain location, pickup or delivered terms, mix specification, and publication date. A regional example does not become a national freight charge. Sources are named in text; their external URLs are collected here.

## What does verification mean?

**A verified record is a retrieved value checked against its stated source and unit.**

It is not a site inspection, a local bid, an engineering approval, or a product guarantee. Missing figures remain unavailable.
