## How do I repair cracks in a concrete driveway?

**Repair driveway cracks with a product selected for the measured crack and surface orientation.**

Measure crack width and depth before buying filler. Remove loose material and contaminants as directed by the product label. A flexible filler seals a crack; a patch compound repairs a different missing-material scope. Slab replacement addresses failed slabs rather than merely covering the opening. The [Concrete Driveway Repair Cost](/concrete-driveway-repair-cost/) separates crack filling, resurfacing, and replacement costs. The selected product’s instructions explain how to repair driveway cracks within its stated application limits.

## How do I repair large cracks in a concrete driveway?

**Large or moving cracks require assessment before cosmetic filling.**

Measure any displacement and inspect the adjacent slab and drainage. Select a product only after confirming the substrate remains suitable for that repair. A backer rod controls the sealant depth where the product instructions require it. The [Driveway Crack Filler](/driveway-crack-filler/) compares filler applications rather than treating every crack material as interchangeable.

## Which filler dimensions apply?

**Each manufacturer sets the dimensions for its own concrete crack product.**

Sakrete Concrete Crack Filler lists cracks up to 1/2 inch wide and 1/4 inch deep. QUIKRETE’s retrieved Concrete Crack Seal sheet lists a 1/2-inch maximum width and calls for backer material to limit application depth; other Quikrete project guidance gives a narrower width, so use the current package instructions. Sika’s Sikaflex+ Crack Flex is self-leveling and intended for horizontal applications; its instructions prohibit use on slopes. Do not carry a product’s width limit into a general structural diagnosis.

## How do I finish concrete driveway crack repair?

**Finish the repair by observing the selected product’s curing and weather restrictions.**

Sika lists final cure at 3–5 days and application temperatures of 40–100°F. Quikrete’s retrieved Crack Seal sheet requires no expected rain within 24 hours and allows a second coat after 24 hours. These are product-specific instructions. Sakrete’s page contains conflicting traffic wording; use its current technical sheet rather than the promotional phrase. Confirm the crack geometry, orientation, substrate condition, and current label before starting. Those checks explain how to repair concrete driveway cracks without confusing sealing with slab repair.
