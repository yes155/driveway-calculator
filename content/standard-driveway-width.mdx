## What sets standard driveway width?

**The local access rules and the vehicles using the driveway establish the required arrangement.**

Portland’s residential rule allows 9–12 feet for a single-width driveway serving specified small residential structures. It also allows a double-width driveway up to 20 feet for that category. Those are Portland-specific provisions, not national design standards. Los Angeles regulates the driveway apron by zoning and curb-line measurement; an apron dimension is not interchangeable with interior parking width.

## How does standard driveway size differ from usable parking space?

**Usable parking needs room for the vehicle and the intended movement around it.**

A boundary measurement does not establish door clearance or turning room. Record fixed edges, garage openings, landscaping and obstacles. Distinguish the one-car access lane from a two-car parking pad. A three-car arrangement needs a layout check instead of automatically multiplying a narrow lane width.

## Where does a turnaround fit in the measurement?

**Include a turnaround as a distinct measured section when it lies outside the main driveway rectangle.**

Mark the perimeter and avoid counting overlapping areas twice. For a taper, record the width at each end. ## What belongs in the before-order checklist?

**Confirm measured boundaries, intended vehicles, drainage and local access requirements.**

Check whether the contractor’s dimensions describe the interior surface, curb opening or flared approach. Preserve pedestrian access and required clearances according to local rules. Ask the installer to explain the proposed grade and where runoff exits. Source-linked local examples illustrate the differences between jurisdictions; they do not authorize construction elsewhere.
