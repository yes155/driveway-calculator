## How much does a concrete slab cost?

**Concrete slab cost per square foot multiplies the measured slab area by the selected installed range.**

HomeGuide lists $6–$12 per square foot for installation. Angi lists ordinary slabs at $4–$8, and Concrete Network lists plain slab work at $6.50–$10.50. Driveway Calculators selects HomeGuide’s range without averaging the sources. The cost of concrete slab installation is distinct from a supplier’s price for ready-mix material.

## How much does a concrete pad cost?

**Concrete pad cost uses the same area calculation, with the pad’s intended use stated in the quote.**

The cost to lay a concrete pad and the cost to install a concrete pad depend on slab thickness, reinforcement, support, and finish. A patio, equipment pad, driveway slab, and garage floor are different applications. Garage slab cost is not a universal substitute for a structural house foundation. Confirm the specified loads, local requirements, subbase, control joints, and drainage with the installer.

## What is the cost to pour a concrete slab?

**The cost to pour a concrete slab includes more than the wet concrete.**

The cost to install concrete slab work includes the quote’s identified formwork, placement, finishing, and curing scope. Additional removal or base work belongs in separately stated items unless included. The cost to pour concrete pad work follows the same scope rule. Thickness changes material volume; it does not justify multiplying every labor and setup cost by the thickness ratio.
