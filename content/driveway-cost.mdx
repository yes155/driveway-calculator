## How much does a driveway cost?

**Selected installed planning ranges are $7–$13 per ft² for asphalt, $6–$10 for standard concrete, $4–$10 for gravel, $10–$30 for generic pavers, and $2–$5 for tar and chip.**

Each range comes from its named source rather than a blended national average. New driveway cost and the cost of a new driveway depend on the material, measured area and included preparation. The comparison above uses one illustrative area and states its limits.

## What changes driveway cost per square foot?

**Material, finished area, base condition, access and surface finish set the installed-work scope.**

A driveway installation quote identifies removal, excavation, compaction, drainage, delivery and cleanup when not included in the installation rate. Driveway paving cost includes the specified surface and supporting layers. The cost to pave a driveway differs between new work and an overlay.

## How much does it cost to build a driveway?

**Building a new driveway includes the chosen installed surface and the site preparation required for that surface.**

Measure the route in sections, establish finished elevations and request a written base and drainage specification. The surface estimator applies sourced rates to the measured area. A site inspection establishes additional project requirements.

## How much should a new driveway cost?

**A new-driveway budget compares local bids for the same area, surface, base preparation, removal, drainage and delivery scope.**

These synonymous budgeting questions use the same installed-cost comparison. Avoid adding work already included in the quoted installed rate. Gravel renewal lacks a comparable fixed national replacement lifespan, so evaluate upkeep separately from upfront cost.
