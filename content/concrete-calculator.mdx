## How to calculate concrete yards?

**To calculate concrete yards, multiply measured area by thickness in feet and divide by 27.**

The concrete slab calculator uses the flat slab footprint and its finished thickness. A concrete pad calculator follows the same geometry; thicker edges or separate footings need their own measured quantities. To understand how to calculate yards of concrete for an irregular outline, calculate its sections and add their volumes. The answer to “how do I calculate yards of concrete?” comes from those dimensions, not the dry weight printed on a bag.

## How do you calculate how much concrete you need?

**Use the measured volume plus the selected ordering allowance.**

Waste allowance accounts for your chosen contingency, while thickness remains the project design. NRMCA discusses ordering additional material; the selected source guidance stays visible. ## How many bags of concrete do I need?

**Divide the required cubic feet by the exact product yield and round up to whole bags.**

The concrete bag calculator uses finished mixed yield, not bag mass divided by a generic concrete density. QUIKRETE Concrete Mix 1101 lists about 0.60 cubic foot for its 80 lb bag. Sakrete’s matching product information provides a cross-check, while other product sizes retain their own label yields. A bag-concrete calculator needs the product name as well as nominal bag weight. The rounded bag count is separate from the unrounded volume.
