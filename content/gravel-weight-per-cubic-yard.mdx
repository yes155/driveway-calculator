## How much does a cubic yard of gravel weigh?

**The weight equals the product’s bulk density multiplied by one cubic yard.**

For a cubic yard of gravel, weight differs between crushed stone, pea gravel and road base. Two Rivers lists 5/8-inch crushed rock at about 2,800 lb per yard. Sutherland lists its AB-minus base at 2,860 lb, while Lyngso lists its virgin Class 2 base at 1.5 US short tons per yard. Each value belongs to that product and source.

## How much does a yard of pea gravel weigh?

**Two Rivers lists its 1/2-inch washed pea gravel at about 2,600 lb per cubic yard.**

Lasalle lists pea gravel at 2,700 lb; Three Z gives approximately 1.4 tons per yard for washed river gravel that includes #8 pea gravel. The answer to “how much does a cubic yard of pea gravel weigh?” therefore needs a matching grade and supplier. The reference table preserves these observations instead of averaging them.

## How much does a yard of 3/4-inch gravel weigh?

**Use the supplier’s conversion for that exact 3/4-inch product.**

Rounded washed rock, clean crushed stone and minus material with fines are different products despite sharing a nominal size. Two Rivers lists its 3/4-inch round washed rock at about 2,600 lb per yard. Rock Pros lists 3/4-inch crush at 2,600 lb in its historical product chart, while Lasalle’s 3/4-inch crush-minus is also 2,600 lb. Matching values do not establish one specification or moisture condition.

## How much does a ton of gravel cover?

**Coverage area equals the cubic feet in one ton divided by the layer depth in feet.**

Coverage depth controls how many square feet does a ton of gravel cover. The generated depth table applies one named density consistently, with no hidden compaction or waste. A finished compacted layer needs the supplier’s loose-to-finished adjustment when the selected weight describes delivered bulk material. Zero adjustment excludes that extra quantity rather than certifying an exact order.
