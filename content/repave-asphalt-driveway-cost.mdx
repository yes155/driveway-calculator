## What is the difference between resurfacing and replacement?

**An overlay adds asphalt over a stable existing pavement; replacement removes the failed surface.**

Ask the contractor to distinguish an overlay from milling and resurfacing in the quote. An overlay leaves the original foundation in service. Tear-out exposes that foundation for inspection and correction. Blacktop Concepts explains that persistent drainage problems, sinking, and structural cracking require evaluation beneath the surface rather than another covering layer.

## How much does it cost to repave a driveway?

The cost to replace asphalt driveway surfacing differs from an overlay because full replacement includes removal in Angi’s benchmark.

**Angi lists an asphalt overlay at $3–$7 per square foot and full replacement at $8–$15 per square foot including removal.**

Angi’s replacement benchmark includes existing-surface removal. Adding another removal charge to that same scope counts the work twice. HomeGuide publishes a separate overlay-only range of $1–$3 per square foot. These figures come from different guides and scopes; they are not averaged into a new national price. Request a written allowance for base repair and drainage work.

## What changes asphalt resurfacing driveway cost?

**Existing pavement condition, preparation scope, and access determine the work being priced.**

Use three quote checks: identify the failing areas, specify the preparation method, and list excluded work. Milling at garage and street transitions is different from removing the entire driveway. Check final elevations and the drainage route before accepting an overlay. Measure the actual repaving area rather than multiplying the entire property frontage.

## When does repair make more sense than repaving?

**Localized damage requires a repair assessment before a whole-driveway replacement budget.**

The asphalt driveway repair cost guide separates cracks, potholes, and patches by their pricing units. The cost calculator applies the selected surface benchmark to your measurements. Sealcoating is a maintenance service, not a substitute for base correction. HomeGuide’s overlay lifespan observation is conditional on a strong foundation; it does not establish a universal service-life guarantee. Compare written bids with matching thickness, preparation, and disposal terms.
