## How much does it cost to seal a driveway?

**Angi’s professional driveway sealcoating benchmark is $1.25–$1.50 per square foot for materials and labor.**

HomeGuide reports $1–$2 per square foot and Fixr reports $0.88–$2.10. Keep these named ranges separate. The answer to how much to seal a driveway depends on the same service scope: product, preparation, coats, and contractor minimum. A small driveway does not necessarily receive a proportionally small bill. Confirm whether crack filling is included.

## How does coverage per gallon change the material order?

**Coverage comes from the named sealer label and the surface condition.**

Latex-ite Ultra Shield lists 400–500 square feet per 4.75-gallon pail per coat on smooth or previously sealed asphalt, and 300–400 on rough or porous asphalt. These are product-specific values. The sealcoat calculator converts measured area and the selected product coverage into an order. It does not substitute professional sealcoating cost per square foot for a material coverage rate.

## How many coats and how much cure time are required?

**The exact product instructions govern coats and cure time.**

Henry HE200’s linked technical sheet specifies 1 coat; Latex-ite Ultra Shield specifies two thin coats. Use the matching label rather than a universal rule for driveway sealing. Keep pedestrians and vehicles off until the chosen product’s traffic instructions are satisfied. Wet appearance alone does not establish readiness, and cool or humid conditions change drying.

## How often is sealing necessary?

**Sealing frequency depends on the product, wear, and pavement condition.**

Published guide intervals conflict, so no universal interval is prefilled. Inspect the surface and confirm its suitability before buying sealer. Sealcoating does not correct a failed base or a pothole. Compare 3 factors in the written quote: preparation, product application, and excluded repairs. The repair guide prices damaged areas; the sealer guide explains material selection without ranking untested products.

Crack filling before sealing is a separate preparation item unless the sealing quote includes it. Coverage per gallon and coats describe product quantity; cure time and sealing frequency follow the selected label and pavement condition.
