## How do you calculate pavers for an area?

**Divide square feet by one paver’s covered area, then apply the entered waste allowance and round up.**

Enter the paver dimensions and use the layout’s actual coverage where the product specifies it. Cuts, borders and pattern modules affect the purchase quantity. A zero allowance excludes extra pavers; it is not a recommendation for a cut-intensive layout.

## What does the paver base calculator measure?

**It measures only the base layer from its footprint and depth.**

Calculate the base separately from the blocks and bedding sand. A base that extends beyond the paved face needs its own measured footprint. The Concrete Masonry and Hardscapes Association connects base design to loading, soil and drainage conditions, so the quantity tool does not prescribe one depth for every driveway or patio.

## How do you calculate paver sand?

**Multiply the sand footprint by the bedding depth in feet and divide by 27 for cubic yards.**

The paver sand calculator estimates bedding sand volume rather than jointing sand coverage. Jointing material requires the exact manufacturer’s coverage for the paver geometry and joint width. Keep its label separate from the bulk bedding layer. Calculate paver sand after deciding which layer the measurement represents.

## Does this paver calculator estimate installed cost?

**No. This calculator outputs paver counts, base and sand quantities; it has no price input or installed-cost result.**

A paver patio cost estimate requires material unit prices, labor and project scope, none of which are calculated here. Driveway loading and base requirements also differ from a pedestrian patio. The [Paver Driveway Cost](/paver-driveway-cost/) gives sourced driveway installation benchmarks alongside material-specific ranges. Include delivery and any actual incremental work without adding ordinary base preparation twice to an installed benchmark.

## What needs checking before buying pavers?

**Confirm the product dimensions, pattern, base specification, sand layer and full-unit rounding.**

Ask the supplier for pallet or pack size and delivery access. Calculate unusual borders and separately measured shapes as their own sections. Record the selected allowance and keep pavers, base and bedding sand distinct so one result does not hide the other order quantities.
