## Which product instructions control the repair?

**Use the directions on the actual bag rather than a universal pothole recipe.**

QUIKRETE Permanent Blacktop Repair describes applications 1–3 inches thick in compacted 1-inch lifts, with repairs limited to about 3 × 3 feet. Sakrete Blacktop Patch’s linked sheet limits repairs to 2 × 2 feet and 4 inches deep. These different limits are not combined into a larger permitted repair.

## How much material does a bag yield?

**Bag yield describes the compacted repair volume, not the driveway area alone.**

QUIKRETE lists about 0.5 cubic foot from a 50-pound bag and about 0.6 cubic foot from a 60-pound bag. Measure the pothole area and depth before comparing package quantities. Loose material appearance does not establish compacted yield. No project bag count is calculated manually in this guide; a generated quantity example requires the matching engine and input specification.

## What does compaction do in the procedure?

**Compaction forms each placed layer into the specified repair.**

QUIKRETE’s instructions require adequately compacted lifts. Sakrete describes placing a 2-inch loose layer and compacting it to 1 inch before repeating. Those directions apply to the named products; do not transfer the loose-depth conversion to another material. Clear the damaged area and follow the selected preparation instructions, then respect the package’s opening-to-traffic requirements.

## Is every cold patch only a temporary repair?

**Repair performance depends on the product and the underlying defect.**

A generic temporary repair description does not erase a manufacturer’s named permanent-repair claim or prove its performance at every site. The reviewed sources establish no universal cold-patch lifespan. Recurrent potholes, alligator cracking, and sinking need attention to the support and drainage. The asphalt driveway repair cost guide distinguishes cold patch, hot patch, and full-depth repairs. Compare three details before buying: permitted dimensions, compacted yield, and application method. Keep the bag label and repair photographs with the maintenance record.
