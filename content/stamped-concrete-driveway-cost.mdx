## What is stamped concrete driveway cost by finish?

**The basic and midrange classes price different decorative work.**

Concrete Network’s basic $10–$14 per square foot covers a single stamp pattern and color. Its $14–$20 midrange covers borders, contrasting patterns, and multiple colors. High-end work starts at $20 per square foot with no published upper bound in that source. Driveway Calculators keeps that class quote-only rather than inventing a capped price range.

## What does the finish specification include?

**The finish specification identifies the stamp pattern, color treatment, release agent, and sealer.**

Ask whether color hardener or integral color is used, how the pattern is applied, and whether the initial sealer is included. Decorative finishes belong in the installed quote, rather than being priced as ready-mix material alone. The [Concrete Driveway Cost in 2026](/concrete-driveway-cost/) explains the base slab installation scope that stamping changes.

## Why do published stamped prices differ?

**Published prices differ because their finish and local job scopes differ.**

HomeGuide lists a broad $8–$26 per square foot range for a stamped driveway including the slab. Angi’s concrete finish table lists stamped work at $12–$18. Local Concrete Contractor publishes $15–$17 for its North Carolina stamped work, including sealer. Those ranges are comparisons, not interchangeable finish tiers. ## What maintenance does stamped concrete need?

**Stamped concrete needs cleaning, crack inspection, and resealing according to the sealer and installer instructions.**

Concrete Network describes resealing as a recurring maintenance task that protects decorative appearance. The research does not establish universal annual upkeep dollars or a finite stamped-only lifespan range. Keep those outputs hidden until a compatible source or project assumption is provided. Before accepting a quote, confirm the pattern and colors, slab and base specification, removal scope, included sealer, and maintenance instructions.
