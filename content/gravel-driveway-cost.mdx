## What changes gravel driveway cost?

**Layer depth, site preparation and haul distance change the amount of material and labor.**

The cost to put in gravel driveway sections on an existing sound base differs from a new build on bare ground. Ask whether the cost to install gravel driveway surfacing includes excavation, grading, compaction and cleanup. LawnStarter reports $1–$3 per square foot, while The Spruce reports $1–$5; those guide ranges remain separate rather than becoming an average.

## How do driveway gravel prices differ by product?

**Supplier prices identify the stone product and delivery terms.**

Crusher run and road base serve structural applications; the surface layer has its own material specification. Pea gravel is rounded and moves differently from angular crushed stone. JMJ’s Lynchburg list prices #57 crushed limestone at $46.75 per ton and crusher run at $39.50 per ton, with delivery extra. These are local examples, not nationwide delivered prices.

## What does gravel delivery cost include?

**Freight charges depend on the supplier’s delivery area, load and minimum order.**

Mourne lists $181 per trip within 15 miles of Pataskala, Ohio, and $203 beyond that distance, with possible additional charges. JMJ lists $75 local delivery within 10 miles of Lynchburg, Virginia. Compare material tons, delivery fee and tax separately; do not treat either supplier fee as a US default.

## How does a gravel driveway cost calculator help before ordering?

**The quantity calculator converts measured sections into cubic yards and tons.**

Calculate the base layer and surface layer separately using the supplier’s density. The gravel driveway calculator supplies the quantity to attach to a quote request; it does not replace installation inspection. Confirm geotextile fabric, edging, drainage and the finished compacted depth before accepting the driveway gravel cost.
