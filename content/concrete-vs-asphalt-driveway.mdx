## What changes concrete vs asphalt driveway cost?

**Removal, base condition and finish change the comparison.**

Compare quotes for the same measured area and included operations. New construction, an asphalt overlay and full replacement are different scopes. Plain broom-finished concrete differs from stamped concrete; asphalt thickness and base preparation also change the work. A low upfront cost does not establish the lower total ownership cost.

## How long does an asphalt driveway last?

**HomeGuide gives an asphalt driveway lifespan of 15–30 years with proper installation and maintenance.**

The interval is a planning range, not a warranty. Drainage, traffic and surface condition affect service life. Asphalt maintenance includes cleaning, inspecting cracks, addressing damaged edges and selecting sealcoating according to the product and site condition. The research does not establish one complete annual upkeep budget.

## How long does a concrete driveway last?

**HomeGuide’s selected concrete driveway lifespan range is 30–40 years under proper installation and maintenance.**

Concrete Network discusses longer examples, and other sources use different bounds; the site retains those differences. Concrete repair depends on whether damage is a shallow surface defect, a moving crack or settling beneath the slab. Filling a crack does not correct failed support.

## How do climate and appearance affect the choice?

**Drainage and the designed pavement layers matter alongside surface color and texture.**

Freeze-thaw exposure, heat and heavy vehicle use belong in the contractor’s design discussion. Neither surface is a universal climate winner. Use the asphalt and concrete cost guides to inspect each scope, then compare all materials in the driveway cost estimator. Keep maintenance expenses separate from upfront cost divided by lifespan; that calculation is not a complete lifetime-cost forecast.
