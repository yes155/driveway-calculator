## What is concrete driveway repair cost by repair?

**Concrete driveway repair uses crack length for filling and affected area for resurfacing or slab work.**

HomeGuide and Angi list concrete driveway crack repair at $0.50–$3 per linear foot. HomeGuide identifies minimum service fees of $100–$250; that is its named planning benchmark, not a fee every contractor charges. HomeGuide’s explanatory resurfacing range is $3–$7 per square foot. Compare the measured damage and scope before choosing a repair line.

## Why do driveway concrete repair costs differ?

**Driveway concrete repair costs differ when the work addresses different defects.**

Crack filling seals a measured crack; resurfacing covers an existing slab surface. Slab leveling and mudjacking address displacement and support rather than cosmetic surface texture. The presence of control joints, active movement, settlement, and drainage problems changes the repair decision. Fixr prices cracked-area filler and sealant at $2–$2.50 per square foot, a different unit and treatment from a linear crack-filling rate.

## When is resurfacing the right scope?

**Resurfacing addresses the surface of a slab that remains structurally sound.**

Angi lists resurfacing at $3–$5 per square foot, while Fixr lists $2.50–$4. Driveway Calculators selects HomeGuide’s named $3–$7 explanatory range and preserves those comparisons instead of averaging them. An overlay does not replace a damaged support system. Obtain an assessment of moving cracks and displaced slabs before selecting surface work.

## When does slab replacement become the relevant comparison?

**Slab replacement becomes the comparison when the proposed repair does not address the slab or support failure.**

The [Cost to Replace a Concrete Driveway](/replace-concrete-driveway-cost/) separates demolition from the new pour and explains bundled quotes. The [Concrete Driveway Cost in 2026](/concrete-driveway-cost/) gives the new installation scope. Before requesting bids, measure crack length and affected area, photograph displacement and control joints, identify drainage symptoms, and confirm each quote’s minimum fee and repair method.
