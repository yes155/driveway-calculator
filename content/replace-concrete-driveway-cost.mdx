## What is the cost to replace a concrete driveway?

**The replacement range is a calculated sum of 2 separately sourced scopes.**

HomeGuide lists standard gray concrete installation at $6–$10 per square foot. Angi lists concrete demolition with dumping fees at $2–$6 per square foot. Adding those ranges produces $8–$16 per square foot before additional work. This is a transparent derived benchmark, not a surveyed national replacement range. The worked example uses the calculator function to generate the total for the entered area.

## What does demolition include?

**The selected demolition benchmark includes breaking up the slab and disposal.**

Angi’s range covers its stated slab scope; depth, reinforcement, equipment access, hauling distance, and disposal terms affect a local bid. Confirm whether hauling and disposal are bundled with demolition. HomeGuide’s broader removal range is $3–$8 per square foot; Fixr lists $2–$6. These differences remain visible in the source comparison instead of being averaged.

## What does the new pour include?

**The new pour replaces the slab after the existing surface is removed and the support is assessed.**

Ask the contractor to identify subbase work, slab thickness, reinforcement, finish, formwork, control joints, and curing. The [Concrete Driveway Cost in 2026](/concrete-driveway-cost/) explains the new installation benchmark. Failed support or drainage needs a separate site assessment; the research does not establish a universal base-work surcharge.

## How do I avoid paying for removal twice?

**Leave demolition out of the added extras when the replacement quote already includes it.**

ZBL Concrete’s Lockport-area $13–$16 per square foot includes tear-out, hauling, grading, rebar, and sealer. That local package is a comparison example, not a national default. Enter either separate removal and installation rates or an all-in replacement quote. The [Concrete Driveway Cost Calculator](/concrete-driveway-cost-calculator/) calculates measurements and quantities so the quote uses the same area. Before ordering, confirm the demolition area, disposal scope, subbase repair, new slab specification, and delivery access.

Slab replacement includes demolition and a new pour. Ask whether hauling and disposal are already included before adding a removal charge.
