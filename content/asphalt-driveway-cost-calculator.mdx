## How does the asphalt driveway cost calculator use measurements?

**The estimate multiplies measured area by the selected low and high installed rates.**

Enter each driveway section in feet. Tapered widths and flat-distance slope measurements change the measured area. The driveway paving cost calculator uses the same area engine as the asphalt quantity tool, keeping the measurements consistent. Planned thickness and producer density calculate tonnage separately; they do not create an unsupported price coefficient.

## Which editable assumptions change the estimate?

**Selected extras change the range only when the entered charge applies outside the installation scope.**

The cost tier selector defaults to a baseline with no regional adjustment. Published low-cost and high-cost regional driveway multipliers were not established, so those choices remain unavailable. Use separately quoted removal, base preparation, and delivery charges. Installation ranges already including those items stay unchanged. Your quoted low and high unit rates replace the published benchmark when selected.

## What does the low and high range include?

**Angi’s selected new-asphalt range includes installation and excludes major leveling or grading.**

Other guides publish different scopes and ranges; their observations remain separate in Methodology. The source name and retrieval date accompany the result. A supplier material price per short ton is not an installed project price. Measure the driveway, check thickness and traffic requirements with the paving provider, and compare written work inclusions before ordering.
