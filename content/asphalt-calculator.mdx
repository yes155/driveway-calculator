## How to calculate asphalt tonnage?

**Multiply area by thickness divided by 12 and by density, then divide by 2,000.**

The complete formula is tons = area × (thickness / 12) × density / 2,000. A short ton (2,000 lb) is the US mass unit used here. An asphalt tonnage calculator handles these unit changes without treating inches as feet. Calculate the asphalt surface and each asphalt base course separately when their depths or mix densities differ.

## How do you choose asphalt density and thickness?

**Use the supplier’s in-place density and the finished compacted thickness.**

The Asphalt Institute publishes an in-place density range of 142–148 lb per cubic foot and uses 148 for quantity estimates. Craig Paving uses 148; the Minnesota Asphalt Pavement Association uses a compacted spread factor of 110 lb per square yard per inch. These named assumptions differ. The editable starting density follows the Asphalt Institute rather than averaging the sources. Colorado Asphalt Pavement Association guidance uses 3 inches of asphalt over an aggregate base for its residential design. Its full-depth asphalt sections are separate designs, so this asphalt driveway calculator does not choose an engineered pavement section for your site.

## How to figure asphalt tonnage for a tapered driveway?

**Use the average of the start and end widths to calculate the footprint.**

Figuring asphalt for a taper uses area = length × (start width + end width) / 2. Measure along the paved slope or enter a flat distance with grade; avoid adjusting a slope measurement a second time. For curves and irregular aprons, divide the footprint into measured sections and add their results. The how-to-measure steps explain how to figure out asphalt tonnage from those separate areas.

## How to calculate tons of asphalt to order?

**Apply your entered waste allowance to the calculated quantity, then round up to the purchase increment.**

Compaction is already represented by compacted thickness and in-place density. Adding another automatic compaction multiplier counts that change twice. Zero waste allowance excludes extra material; it does not recommend an exact order. Ask the plant to confirm the order increment, truck payload and access. Delivery load rounding is separate from the amount of material in the pavement. The unrounded figure explains how to figure tons of asphalt; the rounded figure is the purchasing quantity.
