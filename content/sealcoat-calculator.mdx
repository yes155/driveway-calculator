## How much sealer do I need?

**Use area × coats / coverage per gallon to calculate gallons.**

Container count equals gallons divided by container size, rounded up. Keep the exact gallon quantity visible beside the full-container order. A driveway sealer calculator needs the product label because different sealers cover different areas and some require only 1 coat.

## Which product coverage does this asphalt sealer calculator use?

**Coverage comes from the named product and the existing surface condition.**

Latex-ite Ultra Shield lists 400–500 square feet per 4.75-gallon pail per coat for smooth or previously sealed asphalt, and 300–400 for rough, porous or never-sealed asphalt. The product options use the lower coverage bound for planning and retain both endpoints in the reference. A 4.75-gallon pail is not a 5-gallon container. Henry 200’s linked technical sheet gives 250–400 square feet per 5-gallon pail, while its webpage gives 250–500; that conflict stays visible. SealMaster Ultra gives 70–82 square feet per gallon per coat for its factory blend.

## How many coats belong in the calculation?

**Use the coat count from the selected label rather than one universal setting.**

Latex-ite recommends two thin coats with 24 hours before recoating. Henry 200’s linked sheet says 1 coat only. SealMaster Ultra calls for at least 2 coats, with each coat dry before the next. A concentrate coverage figure is not interchangeable with a gallon of mixed material. Select custom coverage when your product or application differs.

## What needs checking before buying sealcoat?

**Confirm the exact product, container volume, pavement texture and application conditions.**

A quantity result does not establish a drying deadline. Each label sets its own temperature, rain window and traffic time. Clean the surface and complete the label’s crack and patch preparation before sealing.
