## How are cracks, patches, and potholes priced?

**Crack length and patch area use different units.**

Measure each damaged area before requesting a quote. HomeGuide reports $100–$400 per pothole, while Angi groups method-dependent patches at $100–$500. Those project figures do not convert directly into a square-foot rate without a defined area and depth. Ask whether the quoted total includes the crew’s service minimum and whether multiple repairs share that mobilization charge.

## What is the difference between cold patch and hot patch?

**Cold patch uses a packaged repair mix; hot patch uses hot asphalt placed and compacted by a paving crew.**

Product instructions govern the repair depth, lift thickness, and compaction method for a packaged mix. A cold patch figure covers that repair method, not new driveway installation. HomeGuide’s hot-patch benchmark is a method-specific area price. Keep the material bag price, patching labor, and complete job quote separate when comparing bids.

## Does crack filler repair alligator cracking?

**A crack filler seals a crack; it does not replace a failed pavement foundation.**

Blacktop Concepts identifies interconnected cracking, sinking, and repeated potholes as reasons to inspect the base and drainage. Alligator cracking describes a connected pattern rather than a single measured line. Record its affected area and take photographs for the contractor. Adding sealer over that pattern does not establish a structural repair specification.

## When does replacement enter the comparison?

**Replacement enters the comparison when damage extends beyond isolated repair areas.**

Compare 3 scope items: removal of failed material, correction beneath the surface, and replacement pavement thickness. A repair estimate needs the same clarity as a repaving estimate. The crack filler guide covers compatible materials, and the asphalt cold patch guide explains product limits. The repaving guide distinguishes overlay from tear-out. Choose the comparison that matches the actual failure instead of applying one repair price to the whole driveway.

Sealing maintains the surface; it does not replace patching a failed area or repairing the base.
