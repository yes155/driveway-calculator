## Design decision and implementation

The supplied one-page preview is a visual reference, not replacement calculator code. The real shared calculator, source prices, formulas, validation, measurement handoffs and all 51 mapped URLs remain intact. The softer yellow-green accent is #D3E2A9, deep olive is #5F6F32 and light explanation panels are #F0F2E9. Charcoal #1D1E1B and off-white #FAFAF8 remain. The existing `--lime` token is retained as a compatibility name, with its approved softer value. The road favicon uses that same accent. Natural material illustrations retain their material colors.

Calculate and primary output values retain accent emphasis. The header calculator-directory link becomes neutral, page-type labels and process/tool-card surfaces become quieter, and ordinary article links use deep olive. Expanded calculator details use a light surface with dark secondary values, headings, formulas and table text. No input/result layout limits or calculation state handling are replaced by the simplified mockup.

The shared footer now uses a conventional four-column desktop directory: a compact brand/description column, Calculators, Driveway costs, and About & help. A separate bottom row contains copyright and Privacy/Terms/Disclosure. The previous standalone intro row, repeated trust labels, full-width disclaimer and unused watermark rules are removed. All previous footer destinations are retained. Tablet and phone layouts reflow the groups; mobile links have 44px minimum hit heights. The wordmark explicitly uses white text on charcoal rather than inheriting a host heading color.

The footer's two factual sentences are: “Driveway cost estimates and material quantities in US units.” and “Planning estimates. Confirm site conditions and work scope locally.” These state the existing site task and scope, without inventing approvals, contact channels, social accounts, newsletter capability or guarantees.
